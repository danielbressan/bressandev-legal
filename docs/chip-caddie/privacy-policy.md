# Chip Caddie Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 6, 2026

Chip Caddie is developed by Bressan Dev. This Privacy Policy explains how Chip Caddie handles information when you use the app.

## Summary

Chip Caddie is designed as a local-first golf practice app. The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send your practice data to Bressan Dev or any third-party service.

## Information You Enter in the App

Chip Caddie lets you enter golf practice information such as:

- Club names
- Clock-system swing positions
- Shot distances
- Shot history used to calculate averages, ranges, spread, and recommendations

This information is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to Bressan Dev, sold, shared, or used for advertising.

## Data Collection

Chip Caddie does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not currently use:

- User accounts or login
- Cloud sync
- Analytics SDKs
- Advertising SDKs
- Third-party tracking
- Location services
- Camera or photo library access
- Contacts access
- Push notifications

## Local Storage

Chip Caddie stores app data locally on your device using on-device storage. This local data is used only to provide app features, such as your club list, shot history, distance chart, and recommendations.

If you delete the app from your device, the locally stored app data is expected to be deleted as part of the normal app removal process.

## Third-Party Services

Chip Caddie does not currently send your app data to third-party services.

The app is distributed through app stores, and those stores may process information under their own privacy policies when you download, install, purchase, update, or review the app.

## Children's Privacy

Chip Caddie is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, Bressan Dev does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Chip Caddie.

## Changes to This Policy

Bressan Dev may update this Privacy Policy if Chip Caddie's features or data practices change. If the app later adds features such as accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, support uploads, ads, or third-party data services, this policy will be updated to explain those changes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Bressan Dev using the developer contact information listed on the app store page for Chip Caddie.
